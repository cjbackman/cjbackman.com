## Review: Goals 2025

- **[ <span style="font-size: 10px;background-color: lightcoral;">HEALTH</span> ] _Boost performance:_ Run a half marathon in under 1h45m by the end of May 2025.** Completion: <span style="color: green;">100% (goal achieved - 1h36m54s - on May 4, 2025).</span>

I derived a 3 month workout plan together with ChatGPT with the objective to run a half-marathon under 1h45m and the constraint of 3 runs per week. It worked out well.

- **[ <span style="font-size: 10px;background-color: darkseagreen;">GROWTH</span> ] _Learn in public:_ Publish 6 blog posts by the end of 2025.** Completion: <span style="color: green;">100% (goal achieved on Oct 19, 2025).</span>

Started off the year strong, but then I lost momentum again. I didn't have the mindspace to write nor did I prioritize it properly. As I was ramping down work in Q4 the desire to write and inspiration returned however.

  I did a B2 course early in the year but failed to prioritize this topic sufficiently. I'll need to review the goal for next year and reflect on how I can increase the probability of success.

  - **Listen to 52 [History of the Germans](https://open.spotify.com/show/7cvGmixy8K3LD4AkWNbjMH?si=552676f719e541f2) episodes by the end of 2025.** Completion: <span style="color: green;">100% (goal achieved on Feb 16, 2025).</span>

  The key to success here was to add this goal to my habit tracker, because after I removed it upon completion I quickly forgot about the podcast and lost the habit. It was also important to find a space in my week when I could listen without making extra time for it. The answer was during my weekly long Sunday run.
